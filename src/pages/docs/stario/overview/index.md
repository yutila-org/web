Stario is an Android launcher built for extreme minimalism and utility. It provides a clean, ad-free interface featuring local widgets, Material You integration, and built-in search, completely free of background telemetry and bloat.

Following the retirement of upstream development, Stario is maintained and compiled directly under the Yutila organization.

## Operational Philosophy

The system is designed to streamline daily interactions while remaining lightweight and deeply integrated into the open-source ecosystem. 

Modern mobile interfaces are frequently compromised by adversarial elements: bloatware, advertisements, algorithmically driven feeds, and invasive background telemetry. Stario systematically eliminates these vectors to restore a predictable, utility-first user experience.

## Maintenance & Governance

The project operates under rigid open-source licensing and CI/CD policies. This includes:
- Mandatory binary reproducibility audits.
- Complete dependency transparency (SBOM generation).
- Automated SAST and secret scanning pipelines.

We maintain an absolute commitment to keeping the software independent, uncompromised, and freely available to those who choose control over their hardware.
